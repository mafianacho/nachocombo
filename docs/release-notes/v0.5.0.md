## What's new in 0.5.0

- **Nacho Pet.** Nacho, the Nachotown character, now sits in the bottom-right corner of the screen from the first
  launch (on by default; drag it anywhere, right-click ▸ 나초 숨기기 to hide it). It needs no permission and connects to
  nothing until you click it. Combos never switch it on or off.
- **Chat with Nacho.** Click Nacho to type or dictate in Korean; answers stream in and can be stopped. The 연결 tab shows
  what your Nacho is connected to.
- **Nacho account login.** Sign in once with your Nacho account at auth.nachotown.com in the system's browser sheet
  (PKCE); Nacho Combo never sees the password, and the session is kept in the Keychain.
- **Capture and shelf → Nacho.** Right-click a floating capture ▸ Ask Nacho About This…, right-click a shelf item ▸
  Give to Nacho, or drop files on Nacho. Nacho looks at pictures and reads text files.
- **Arranges for you.** Ask Nacho to snap the front window ("창 왼쪽으로 붙여 줘") or switch a gadget on or off
  ("킵 어웨이크 켜 줘"). It never switches Computer Use or itself and never turns on a gadget that still needs a
  permission; you can switch this off on Nacho Pet's Settings tab.
- **The Nacho character is not open source.** The source code stays MIT; the character pictures, app icon, logo and
  names are not covered (캐릭터 그림은 오픈소스 대상이 아니며 무단 사용 금지). See
  [TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/mafianacho/nachocombo/blob/v0.5.0/TRADEMARKS.md).

**Not yet tried on a real Mac.** Nacho Pet's login, chat, uploads and commands were tested end to end against a mock
server and the app was built in CI, but this version has not been run on a Mac. **Signing in to the real Nacho server
starts working once the server-side login setup for Nacho Combo is in place;** until then the login sheet shows an
error and the rest of Nacho Combo works as before.

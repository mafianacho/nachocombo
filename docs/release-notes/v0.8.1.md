## What's new in 0.8.1

- **The chat follows Nacho.** Drag the pet while its chat is open and the chat comes along on every move and where you
  drop it. It keeps the same place next to the pet as when it opens: above it with the tail on the pet, below it near
  the top of the screen, always on the screen the pet is on. What you are typing, the cursor and an answer that is
  still arriving stay as they are.
- **Resize the chat.** Drag the chat's left edge, the edge away from the pet (top, or bottom when the chat is below
  the pet) or the corner between them. The right edge, lined up with the pet, and the tail side stay put.
  - The smallest size is 320 × 420; the largest is what fits on the screen. While you drag, it grows only into the room
    on the pet's side, so it does not jump to the other side of the pet.
  - The header, the conversation, the notice banner, the files waiting to be sent and the input row all follow the new
    width; answers get wider with the chat.
  - The size is kept for next time, also after quitting and opening Nacho Combo again. If a saved size does not fit
    next to the pet where it is now, the chat is shown as tall as the room allows and gets its full size back when
    there is room.
  - **대화창 크기 원래대로** in the pet's right-click menu puts it back to the standard 360 × 520.

Your settings stay as they were; the chat starts at the standard size until you resize it.

**Not yet tried on a real Mac.** Where the chat goes for each pet position, the size limits, and saving and restoring
the size are covered by tests on Linux and macOS, and the real chat window was drawn off screen next to a pet on the
macOS CI machine (before and after a drag, flipped below the pet, large and small, restored after a relaunch).
Dragging the pet and the chat's edges with a mouse, several displays and Retina screens have not been checked on a Mac.

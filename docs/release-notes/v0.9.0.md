## What's new in 0.9.0

### Answer Nacho's questions in the chat
- **Every option has a number.** Press its number key while the text field is empty (Enter answers, Esc clears), click
  it, or say it.
- **Approvals:** the card says what Nacho wants to do, the result (merchant, amount …) and whether it can be undone, with
  **실행** above **거절** (same size). A number alone never runs anything: number + Enter, or a click.
- **One choice:** numbered rows, a click answers. **Several:** tick rows, then **확정**.
- **Suggestions:** quick-reply chips under Nacho's answer; a click sends that text.
- **Rule proposals:** the rule in one sentence with **적용** / **아니요**.
- The card shows when the answer is on its way (no double click), a failure with **다시 시도**, the row you chose once
  it is answered, and when it expired (time in KST). Logins, codes and payment cards still go to the phone app.
- **Big tasks start again.** A task the server holds until you confirm it ("약 N~M크레딧 · 확인 후 시작해 주세요") now shows
  a card with **시작** and **취소** instead of a grey line, and the header says "시작 확인 필요" instead of "곧 시작해요".
  If the estimate changed meanwhile, the card shows the new one before you press again.

### Hold Nacho to talk
- Press Nacho and keep still: a ring fills over about 2 seconds, then Nacho listens (listening face, a ring that follows
  your voice). Let go: what you said is written down by the Nacho server and sent by itself as your message.
- Let go earlier = the usual click (chat opens or closes); move it = the usual drag; Esc or dragging off Nacho cancels.
- The first time, macOS asks for the microphone. If it is refused, the chat says so with a link to the settings.
- **Spoken replies:** when you spoke, the first sentence of Nacho's answer is read with the Mac's built-in Korean voice
  (no extra cost). Settings ▸ Nacho Pet ▸ Voice switches it off; typing or stopping silences it at once.
- Writing down what you said needs the Nacho server's dictation to be on. Until it is, holding Nacho records but the
  chat tells you dictation is not available.

### Nacho can do more on this Mac (15 actions)
- Open, switch to and hide apps; list running (and installed) apps.
- List windows per display and arrange up to 8 windows at once, also across displays.
- Sound volume and built-in display brightness (Do Not Disturb and external monitors: Nacho explains why not).
- Focus Dim strength, empty the File Shelf (undoable).
- **Workspaces:** save running apps, window places and gadget states under a name ("업무 모드") and put them back later;
  putting one back opens missing apps and never quits any. The Nacho Pet settings list them with Delete.
- In the chat these actions now show their Korean names instead of "도구".
- Quitting apps is not offered yet: it stays off until the server asks for your approval first.
- Windows and workspaces need the Accessibility permission; without it Nacho says how to allow it.

Your settings stay as they were. Spoken replies are on by default.

**Not yet tried on a real Mac.** The card rules, the hold timing (click, drag, cancel) and spoken-reply rules are
covered by tests on Linux and macOS, and the real chat window, cards and ring were drawn off screen on the macOS CI
machine in light and dark. The desktop actions ran on the CI Mac (open, switch, hide, arrange, workspaces, volume).
Holding Nacho with a mouse, the microphone and its permission dialog, the spoken voice, and several displays have not
been checked on a Mac.

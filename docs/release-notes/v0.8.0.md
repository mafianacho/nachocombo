## What's new in 0.8.0

- **Selection bar for Nacho Pet.** Select text in any app and a small bar appears next to it: 복사 · 검색 · 번역 ·
  요약 · 설명 | 나초에게 묻기 · ⋯.
  - 복사 and 검색 stay on your Mac. 검색 opens Google in your default browser; Naver or DuckDuckGo can be chosen in
    Settings.
  - 번역, 요약, 설명 and 나초에게 묻기 (a one-line question) open the pet's chat with a preview of exactly what will be
    sent. The preview lists the selected text (up to 8,000 characters; it tells you when a longer selection is cut),
    the app, the window title, the page in a browser (its address without the part after `?` or `#`), a little text
    before and after the selection (up to 400 characters each side), and a language guess. You can take out any row,
    and put it back, before you send. **Nothing is sent until you press 보내기.**
  - What you send goes to Nacho as one attached note (`선택 맥락.md`); the message itself only says what to do. Nacho
    streams the answer in the chat bubble, with 복사 · 다시 · 보낸 내용 보기 under it.
  - ⋯ has "never in this app", "pause for an hour" and the settings.
- **Off until you switch it on.** The bar is part of Nacho Pet, not a tool of its own. Switch it on from the card in
  the pet's chat or in **Nacho Pet ▸ Settings ▸ Selection Bar**, where you can also reorder or hide buttons, choose when
  the bar shows (after 0.3 s, right away, after 0.6 s, or only while holding ⌘) and the search engine, and manage the
  apps it never shows in.
- **Accessibility permission.** Reading the selection needs Accessibility. Nacho Combo asks for it only when you switch
  the bar on, never at launch. Until it is allowed, the bar stays off and Settings says so.
- **Where it never shows.** Password fields, while secure input is on, Nacho Combo's own windows, 1Password, Bitwarden,
  Keychain Access, Passwords and System Settings, terminals (Terminal, iTerm, Warp; each can be allowed in Settings),
  and the apps you add. In those places the selected text is not even read. Selected text is never written to logs.
- **Pet badges.** The pet no longer shows a badge while answering (the chat header says it). The dictation, thinking
  and dozing badges now say what they mean as a tooltip and to VoiceOver.

Your settings stay as they were; the selection bar starts off.

**Signing in to the production server** works once the server side has the Nacho Combo login client set up. Until
then, signing in to production does not complete, so the bar's requests to Nacho cannot be sent there yet; 복사 and
검색 work without signing in.

**Not yet tried on a real Mac.** The selection bar's rules (what is read, what is sent, the limits, where it never
shows, nothing sent before 보내기, no selected text in logs) are covered by tests on Linux and macOS, and its AppKit
views were drawn off screen on the macOS CI machine to compare them with the design. Watching selections in other
apps, the Accessibility prompt and how much of a page address each browser exposes have not been checked on a Mac.

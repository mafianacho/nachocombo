## If you fork
1. Give your app a different name and bundle identifier.
2. Delete `Resources/Nacho/` and `Resources/AppIcon.png` (and `docs/images/app-icon.png`) and use your own artwork.
   Without `Resources/Nacho/` the pet gadget shows a plain fallback instead of the character.
3. Keep the MIT copyright notice for the code, as the license asks.
